❌ The Problem

Villa owners in Bali receive booking inquiries from multiple channels — Instagram DM, WhatsApp, website, email. They have to manually reply to each one, often missing or delaying responses. This leads to lost bookings and unhappy guests.

  • Manual reply to every inquiry — slow and error-prone
  • Staff doesn't get notified immediately
  • No centralized record of all inquiries
  • Guests wait hours (or days) for a response

✅ The Solution

An automated system that handles the entire inquiry process: captures data, saves it, responds to the guest, and notifies staff — all within seconds of form submission.

  • Instant confirmation email to guest (< 30 seconds)
  • Real-time Telegram notification to staff
  • All data centralized in Google Sheets
  • Works 24/7 — even at 3 AM
